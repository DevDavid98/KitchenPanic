# Kitchen Panic

## Style

* **Visual Style:** Low-poly / comedic
* **Perspective:** First-person
* **Game Level:** Restaurant / Kitchen

## Objective

The player's objective is to make a certain amount of money within a time limit.

* The required amount of money increases each day.
* The player must meet the money quota for the entire week to win.

### Winning Objectives

The player must:

* Make enough money by the end of each day.
* Make enough money by the end of the week.
* Stay alive.
* Fulfill customer orders.
* Successfully defend the kitchen.

### Failing Conditions

The player loses if:

* The player dies.
* Too many orders are made incorrectly.
* No orders are fulfilled.
* The player does not make enough money by the end of the week.
* If the player does not meet the weekly money requirement, the player explodes.

## Cooking Mechanic

The basic cooking process:

**Combine 3 ingredients → Cooking Timer → Final Product → Give Food to Customer**

The ingredients used can affect what happens when the customer receives the food.

---

## Health Mechanic

The player can take damage from:

* Enemies
* Customers
* Environmental hazards
* Food effects

The player can regain health:

* Slowly over time
* By eating customer food

---

## Guns

The player has access to:

* Pistol
* Machine Gun

Weapons have **unlimited ammunition**.

**The player cannot cook while holding a weapon.**

---

## Timer

### Order Timer

When a customer places an order, the order timer starts.

If the player gives the correct food to the customer before the timer expires:

* The customer pays.
* The customer eats the food.
* The food may cause a special effect depending on what was made.

If the player does not complete the order in time:

* The customer becomes violent.
* The customer may attack or shoot the player.

### World Timer

Each working day lasts approximately **5–10 minutes**.

# Food & Ingredients

Ingredients can have different effects depending on how they are used.

Possible effects include:

* Setting the customer on fire.
* Exploding.
* Creating a smell or other comedic effect.
* Causing other unexpected effects.

Ingredients can also have effects when dropped.

Some ingredients have a random chance to:

* Explode.
* Cause an effect.
* Create another unexpected hazard.

---

# Ragdolling

Ragdolling can be caused by food effects.

When certain food effects occur, they can affect:

* The player
* NPCs
* Customers
* Enemies

These effects can cause the affected character to ragdoll.

---

# Dialogue Box

NPCs and enemies can randomly generate dialogue.

The dialogue should be:

* Funny
* Random
* Comedic
* Appropriate to what is happening

Examples can include NPCs reacting to:

* Bad food
* Explosions
* Fire
* Being attacked
* Other unexpected events

---

# Back Door

The kitchen has a back door with its own health.

Enemies in the back alley will attempt to break through the door.

If the back door's health reaches zero:

* Enemies can enter the kitchen.
* Enemies will attack the player.
* Enemies may shoot or physically attack the player.
* The player must rebuild the door.

The player can repair the back door to prevent enemies from entering.

---

# Fire

Random fires can start inside the kitchen.

While a fire is active:

* The player cannot cook.
* The player must deal with the fire before continuing to cook.

Fires act as an unexpected hazard that interrupts the player's normal workflow.

---

# Fire Extinguisher

The player can use a fire extinguisher to put out fires.

**Fire → Grab Fire Extinguisher → Extinguish Fire → Resume Cooking**

---

# Enemies

There are three main types of enemies:

* Customers
* Giant Roaches
* Giant Rats

---

## Customers

Customers normally enter the restaurant to order food.

However, customers can become hostile.

A customer may shoot or attack the player if:

* The wrong order is given.
* The order timer runs out.

Some customers may have different reactions depending on the food they receive.

---

## Giant Roaches

Giant roaches spawn randomly in the back alley.

Their goal is to:

* Reach the back door.
* Break through the door.
* Enter the kitchen.
* Attack the player.
* Take food.

Characteristics:

* Low health.
* Spawn frequently.
* Large numbers.
* Easier to kill individually.

---

## Giant Rats

Giant rats also spawn randomly in the back alley.

Their goal is to:

* Reach the back door.
* Break through the door.
* Enter the kitchen.
* Attack the player.
* Take food.

Characteristics:

* High health.
* Spawn less frequently.
* More difficult to kill.
* More dangerous than giant roaches.

---

# Player Weapon Restriction

The player **cannot cook while holding a weapon**.

The player must switch between:

**Weapon → Combat**

and

**No Weapon → Cooking**

This forces the player to choose between defending the kitchen and preparing customer orders.

# Game Themes / Jam Requirements

## Qué Raro! — "How Strange!"

Add something strange, absurd, or unexpected to the game.

Possible examples:

* Food randomly exploding.
* Customers suddenly becoming violent.
* NPCs saying ridiculous dialogue.
* Food causing strange effects.
* The player exploding for failing the weekly quota.

## Cuidado — "Be Careful"

Add an unexpected hazard, consequence, or twist that players have to learn to navigate.

Possible examples:

* Random kitchen fires.
* Explosive ingredients.
* Enemies attacking through the back door.
* Customers becoming hostile when orders are late.
* Food causing unexpected effects.
* Multiple hazards happening at the same time.
